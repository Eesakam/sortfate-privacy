Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you play SortFate.

Effective date: 1 November 2026

1. About SortFate

SortFate is a mobile ball-sorting puzzle game for Android. This policy describes the app's current intended release, without advertisements or analytics integrations.

2. Information stored on your device

SortFate saves game information locally on your device so that you can continue playing. This may include level progress, unfinished puzzles, earned coins, power-up tokens, unlocked or purchased in-game cosmetics, achievements, and game preferences such as audio settings.

These game records are intended to remain on your device and are not associated with a SortFate user account. The current release does not require you to provide your name, email address, or other personal details to play.

3. Data collection and sharing

The current SortFate app does not intentionally send your gameplay history or locally saved progress to the developer's servers and does not include developer-integrated advertising or analytics services.

Your device, operating system, app store, or distribution platform may independently process technical information, such as app installation data or diagnostic information, under their own terms and privacy policies. This policy does not govern those separate services.

4. Internet access and advertisements

Core gameplay is designed to work offline. This release does not contain rewarded advertising, banner advertising, or advertising-based token rewards. If advertisements or connected services are added in a later release, this policy and any applicable consent or disclosure processes will be updated before those features are introduced.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally saved game data generally remains on your device until you remove it using applicable app or Android storage controls, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling or clearing app data may permanently remove progress, purchases made using virtual in-game currency, settings, and other local game records. SortFate does not currently provide a cloud-backup account for recovering them.

6. Children's privacy

SortFate does not ask players to create an account or directly submit personal information. The app's intended audience and any applicable age-related requirements will be reflected in the Google Play listing. Parents and guardians should review device and Google Play controls appropriate for their children.

7. Security

Keeping game progress on the device reduces the need to transmit it over the internet. However, no device or storage method is completely secure. Protect your device using its available security features.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if SortFate's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.
